HOW A SHIPMENT MOVES

From harvest
to your warehouse.

  1. 01

    Farm Procurement & Grading

    We buy directly from growers around Nashik and sort by size, skin quality, and moisture content.

  2. 02

    Curing & Storage

    Lots are cured and held in ventilated storage until your shipment date, protecting shelf life in transit.

  3. 03

    Packing & Fumigation

    Bagged to your specification — mesh, jute, or custom weight — and fumigated per destination-country rules.

  4. 04

    Export Documentation

    Phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin, and customs filings are completed before the container is sealed.

  5. 05

    Ocean Freight

    Container moves from JNPT or Mundra to your destination port, tracked the whole way.

  6. 06

    Destination Clearance & Delivery

    We coordinate with your local clearing agent so cargo reaches your warehouse without delay.

ABOUT ONIORA

Grown near Lasalgaon,
shipped worldwide.

Oniora Export Co. is based in Nashik, Maharashtra — home to Lasalgaon, Asia's largest onion market. We work directly with local growers to source, grade, and cure onions to export standard, then handle every certificate and customs step so buyers overseas receive cargo that clears without questions.

We keep the chain short: fewer middlemen between the farm and the container means fresher onions, tighter grading, and pricing that stays competitive season over season.
