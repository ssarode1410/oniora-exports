HOW A SHIPMENT MOVES

From request
to receiving dock.

  1. 01

    Request & Quote

    Tell us origin, destination, cargo type and volume. We return a rated quote within one business day.

  2. 02

    Sourcing & Booking

    We confirm suppliers if needed, book carrier space, and issue the booking confirmation and packing plan.

  3. 03

    Export Customs

    Export declarations, HS classification, and any licenses are filed before the cargo leaves origin.

  4. 04

    Transit

    Ocean or air, tracked door to port with milestone updates and proactive delay handling.

  5. 05

    Import Customs & Duties

    Entry filed on arrival, duties calculated in advance so there are no surprises at release.

  6. 06

    Last-Mile Delivery

    Cleared cargo moves to your warehouse, distribution center, or end customer.

ABOUT ONIORA

Fifteen years of
keeping cargo moving.

Oniora Exports co. was founded on a simple frustration: too many freight forwarders treat documentation as an afterthought, and clients pay for it in delays. We built the business the other way around — compliance and paperwork lead, transportation follows.

Today we operate as an extension of our clients' own logistics teams: one point of contact, transparent rates, and a paper trail that holds up if customs ever asks a second question.
